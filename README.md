
# Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail superstore sales performance using Power BI, SQL, and Excel. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance by tracking sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, shipping efficiency, and sales pipeline performance.

## Objectives

* Analyze sales and profitability trends
* Track customer and segment performance
* Evaluate regional sales performance
* Identify product-level profitability
* Monitor shipping and delivery efficiency
* Track sales pipeline conversion performance

## Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* SQL
* Excel
* DAX

## Key KPIs

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Total Orders
* Customer Count
* Average Delivery Days
* Sales Conversion Rate

## Dashboard Modules

### Sales & Profit Overview

Tracks revenue, profit, and overall performance trends.

### Customer Segment Analysis

Identifies high-value customers and segment-wise profitability.

### Regional Performance Analysis

Analyzes performance across regions and states.

### Product Category Analysis

Tracks top-performing and low-performing products.

### Shipping Performance Analysis

Measures delivery timelines and shipping efficiency.

### Sales Pipeline Analysis

Tracks opportunity stages and conversion funnel.

## Key Insights

* Technology category generated the highest profit.
* Furniture showed lower profitability despite strong sales.
* Certain regions contributed higher revenue but lower profit margins.
* Delivery delays impacted customer experience and order efficiency.
* High discounts reduced profitability across some product categories.

## Business Recommendations

* Optimize discount strategy to improve margins.
* Improve shipping efficiency to reduce delays.
* Focus on profitable product categories and regions.
* Improve sales pipeline conversion rates.
